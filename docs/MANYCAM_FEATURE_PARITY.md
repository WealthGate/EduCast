# ManyCam 9 capability audit

Reviewed on November 9, 2026. The Windows changelog lists ManyCam 9.3.1.8 at the time of review. This audit tracks publicly documented product behavior and additional production requirements from the brief. Each grouped row covers related capabilities with a common implementation and verification path. Outstanding rows describe planned work. Verification measures EduCast behavior.

ManyCam's pricing comparison varies by edition, including source and device limits, Full HD versus 4K, additional virtual cameras, and enterprise user management. Those limits are reference metadata. EduCast sets its own entitlement rules. ManyCam assets, code, and branding are proprietary and are excluded from EduCast.
